01 — My Story

Earned, Not Inherited.

Born on March 23, 1998, in The Nilgiris, DEEPTH built his path alone with no financial net or family backing. Overcoming 20 years of isolation and humiliation, he transformed struggle into creation. From Theatre Arts and Micro-Art to founding circular economy startups and B2B growth agencies, he engineers proof where systems see waste.
