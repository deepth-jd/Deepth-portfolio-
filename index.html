The turning point: soul through art

I found discipline and soul in art: theatre, fine arts and micro-art, including pencil-lead carving, paper carving and threaded portraits. I learned to upcycle waste into functional art, and with it a lesson that now drives my business: nothing is worthless until you decide it is.

The verdict around me was ready: யூஸ்லஸ், பொழைக்க தெரியாதவன் (useless, doesn't know how to survive). I answered with the relentless mindset of Cristiano Ronaldo and a creed of my own: "I'm the best. Never Quit."
