The Story

The Unbroken Journey.

I am a native of The Nilgiris, who migrated to Namakkal 15 years ago. I did not inherit a business, a network, capital, or a ready-made identity.

I built one.

For more than two decades, the path meant navigating isolation, systemic rejection, physical strain, false accusations, and the absence of family, financial, or moral support. Every stage forced me to become more resourceful than the circumstances around me.

Before becoming a founder, I worked across radically different environments: manual labour, car washing, bakery service, BPO front-office management, theatre, writing, training, micro-art, craftsmanship, and dog training.

That range was never random. It taught me how people work, how systems fail, how attention is earned, and how discarded resources can become useful again.

Creativity is not decoration. It is resource conversion.

I have used craft productivity to help redirect young people away from excessive screen time. Waste oil drums became shelters for stray dogs and barbecue stoves. Discarded tyres became furniture. Bottles became planters.

The principle is simple: look again before you throw away.

Venture 01

DEERAH

Waste Reimagined

Turning biological waste into industrial possibility.

DEERAH is a circular bio-economy venture built around the valorization of poultry and butcher waste, including feathers. Instead of treating these materials as a disposal problem, the model explores their transformation into higher-value products without relying on harsh chemicals.
